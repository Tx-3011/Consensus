# Brag Plan: Consensus Trap

## What is this app?
Consensus Trap compares answer surfaces, keeps their citations inspectable, and traces documented source relationships without treating search engines as independent witnesses.

## The angle
Make research disagreement feel like a forensic UI reveal. Consensus Trap starts with a confident-looking answer, then calmly asks what survives the citation trail. The hook is the product's own warning: agreement is observable, but it is not a truth score.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
The phrase "Search surfaces are witnesses, not votes" stamps in above the giant product question: "What survives the citation trail?" The dark editorial interface and amber accent establish the product before the workflow begins.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The question form fills with the default 1918 influenza prompt, then three witnesses lock in: Google AI Mode, Bing Copilot, and Brave AI Mode.
- The Research result view assembles three labeled answer cards, each visibly marked as a fixture answer with citations.
- Claim comparison and the evidence map appear together. The graph routes answer surfaces to documents while the line "A citation is not an inspected source" stays visible.

## Outro / punchline
The graph resolves into a restrained final lockup: "Observed agreement is not a truth score." Then the product's footer principle lands: "Every relationship must remain tied to inspectable evidence."

## User flow worth showing
Enter a factual question -> choose answer witnesses -> collect answer blocks and citations -> compare claims -> inspect the saved evidence map.
